Terms of use - clownworld-rclone

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Who may use it

clownworld-rclone is an internal tool for members of the Clown World team only. It is not offered to the public and is not part of the Clown World shop.

What it does

It copies the team's own working files between the team's two Mac computers and the team's own Google Drive, using the open-source program rclone.

Your Google account

By signing in, a team member lets the app read and write files in their own Google Drive so it can copy the team's files. Access can be removed at any time from Google Account settings.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without any warranty. Keep your own backups of important files.
