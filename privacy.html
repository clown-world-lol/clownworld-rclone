Privacy note - clownworld-rclone

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What the app is

clownworld-rclone is an internal tool run by the Clown World team. It uses the open-source program rclone to copy the team's own working files between the team's two Mac computers and the team's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

When a team member signs in with Google, the app gets permission to read and write files in that team member's Google Drive. It only copies the team's own working folders. It does not read, open or use anyone else's files.

What it collects

Nothing from anyone outside the team. The app has no website sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect names, emails, contacts or any other personal information.

What it shares

Nothing. Files are copied only between the team's own computers and the team's own Google Drive. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any third party.

How it is stored and protected

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the team's own computers. The team can remove the app's access at any time from their Google Account settings (Security, then Third-party apps with account access).

Google user data

clownworld-rclone's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to copy the team's own files, and is never used for advertising or transferred to others.
